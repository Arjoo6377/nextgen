NextGen Broadband — Event & Concert Wi-Fi add-on (4 pages)
=========================================================

HOW TO INSTALL
Unzip this over the root of the website, keeping the folder structure:
  events-wifi.php, concert-hotspot.php, event-projects.php, event-planner.php
  includes/event-helpers.php
  assets/css/events-wifi.css, assets/js/events-wifi.js
  assets/img/event-crowd-illustration.jpg
  assets/img/events/            <- drop your own event photos here (see below)
No existing file is overwritten. The four pages work immediately at their own URLs.

YOUR OWN EVENT PHOTOS
Put .jpg/.png/.webp files in assets/img/events/. A "From the ground" gallery
appears automatically on event-projects.php (file name = caption, e.g.
"Sunburn-main-stage-network-room.jpg"). With no photos, the section stays hidden.
Please only upload photos you took or have the right to use.
